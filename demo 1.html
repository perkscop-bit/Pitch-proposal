01
01 — Why We Exist

The chains have had loyalty
for years. You've had paper stamps.

Every chain in Australia runs a loyalty program. Every aggregator takes 10–20% margin and keeps the customer data. You get paper stamps and an Instagram account. We built Perks Hive to close that gap. For good.

PROB · 01

No Loyalty, Losing Ground

Starbucks, Guzman, Subway. Every chain runs a loyalty program. Your regulars collect points there while you stay invisible. They never forget the chain. They might forget you.

PROB · 02

Nobody Downloads Another App

Ask someone to download an app and 40–60% won't bother. That's half your potential regulars walking out without a card. Perks needs nothing but a QR scan. The card lands straight in Wallet.

PROB · 03

Out of Sight, Out of Mind

70% of loyalty members forget they're enrolled if they can't see the card. A Perks card lives on the lock screen. Every time they check their phone, your brand is there.

PROB · 04

Aggregators Own Your Customers

EatClub takes the commission. Uber Eats keeps the customer record. When someone orders through them, you make the coffee. They keep the relationship. Perks gives it back.

PROB · 05

Dead Hours Bleed Money

Every venue has a slow Tuesday afternoon. Deep-discounting on EatClub trains customers to wait for deals. Perks activates your existing regulars with a personal invite. Not a price drop.

PROB · 06

The Counter is the Bottleneck

Asking staff to read out phone numbers is slow and error-prone. One QR code at the counter does it in 30 seconds with no staff involvement at all. Queue keeps moving.

04 — Perks Moments

Fill slow periods without
discounting to strangers.

Every venue loses money in dead windows: a slow Tuesday afternoon, a rainy Thursday, end-of-day surplus. The only tools available today are deep discounts on EatClub or mystery bags on TGTG. Both destroy margin and brand. Perks Moments is the alternative.

— The Old Way

EatClub / TGTG

Mass-blast strangers with 30–50% discounts. Platform takes 10% commission. Customer becomes loyal to the deal. Not you. When you stop discounting, they vanish.

— Perks Moments

A personal nudge to your regulars

You have a slow afternoon. Happy hour starting in an hour. A cardholder's birthday today. Perks looks at your cardholder history and sends them a personal nudge framed as access, not a deal.

— The Result

Access, not desperation

"Blue Stone Lane has an open afternoon." Your regular gets a personal invitation. They already belong. You fill capacity without signalling desperation or cutting margin. The venue never looks quiet.

The consumer who visits every Thursday at 3pm gets a personal invitation at 2:45pm. Not a mass broadcast to strangers. A private message to someone who already cares.
